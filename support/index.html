Frequently Asked Questions

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your device and never sent to any server. FinBuddy has no backend — your data stays with you.

How do I back up my data?

FinBuddy data is stored in your device's app storage. Enable iCloud Backup (iPhone) or Google One Backup (Android) in your device settings to include FinBuddy data in your device backups.

I deleted the app and lost my data. Can I recover it?

Unfortunately no — since all data is local, uninstalling removes it. If you had a device backup made before uninstalling, restoring that backup should recover your data.

Can I transfer data between devices?

Currently FinBuddy does not support cross-device sync. Data is tied to the device it was entered on. This is a feature we may add in a future update.

Are currency exchange rates real-time?

Exchange rates are fetched periodically and may not reflect live market rates. They are for personal tracking purposes only and should not be used for financial transactions.

How do I delete all my data?

Simply uninstall the app from your device. This permanently removes all locally stored data.
